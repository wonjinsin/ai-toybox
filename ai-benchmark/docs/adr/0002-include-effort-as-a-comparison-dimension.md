# ADR 0002: Include Effort as a Comparison Dimension

Date: 2026-09-24

Status: Accepted

## Context

The planned benchmark comparisons include models and explicitly permitted combinations of skills and tools. The user also wants to understand how effort settings affect benchmark outcomes.

## Decision

Include effort as an explicit comparison dimension alongside models, skills, and tools.

## Consequences

- Benchmark configuration and result records must distinguish the effort settings used, where available.
- Specific effort settings, comparison groups, and time or cost constraints remain undecided.
- This decision does not authorize benchmark execution or additional skills, tools, or agents within benchmark runs.
